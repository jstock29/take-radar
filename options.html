Take Radar

About

Take Radar highlights the people you’re keeping an eye on, on every page you visit. Hover a highlight to see which lists someone is on.

Add a name
Select it on any page, right-click, and choose “Add to Take Radar list”.
Pause
Privacy
Your lists and settings stay in this browser, and page text is only read on your device to find names. The only network requests are the list addresses you subscribe to; the starter lists come from this project’s GitHub page.
