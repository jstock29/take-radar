# Take Radar list: Republican operatives
#
# People whose careers are in Republican politics: strategists, consultants,
# pollsters, campaign, party and super PAC staff, and former White House or
# leadership aides. Many now appear in news stories and on air as analysts,
# pollsters, hosts or "strategists" without that background being obvious.
# Inclusion reflects professional background, not current views: several
# people here have since broken with the party or with Trump, and the note
# says so where that's the case.
#
# Elected officials and household names are mostly left out; the point is
# the people you might not recognize in an article. Names shared with other
# well-known people (e.g. a film director) are left out to avoid false
# matches; the note on each highlight says who is meant.
#
# Corrections and additions: https://github.com/jstock29/take-radar/issues
# Last reviewed: 2026-10
#
# Format: Name | note
